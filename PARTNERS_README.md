# Partners Page - Telecom Security Library

## Overview

The Partners page (`partners.html`) is a comprehensive landing page designed to showcase strategic partnerships, sponsorship opportunities, and industry collaborations for the Telecom Security Library. This page serves as a central hub for potential partners, sponsors, and collaborators to understand partnership opportunities and engage with the organization.

## Features

### 🎯 Strategic Partnership Showcase
- **Research Institutions**: Academic and research partnerships
- **Industry Leaders**: Telecom companies and security vendors
- **Technology Partners**: AI, blockchain, and quantum technology companies
- **Community Partners**: Open source projects and standards organizations

### 💰 Sponsorship Tiers
- **Bronze Sponsor**: $1,000/year - Basic benefits and recognition
- **Silver Sponsor**: $2,500/year - Enhanced benefits and monthly access
- **Gold Sponsor**: $5,000/year - Exclusive access and custom tools
- **Platinum Sponsor**: $10,000/year - Strategic partnership and brand integration

### 🔧 Partnership Opportunities
- **Research Collaboration**: Joint research projects and publications
- **Tool Development**: Collaborative development of security tools
- **Education & Training**: Joint training programs and certifications

### 📊 Partnership Impact Statistics
- 50+ Research Papers
- 25+ Industry Partners
- 100+ Security Tools
- 1000+ Community Members
