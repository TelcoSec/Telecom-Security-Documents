Telecommunications Security Researcher

RFS specializes in telecommunications security research, covering 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G networks, SS7/Diameter protocols, SIM card security, baseband vulnerabilities, and emerging threats in mobile infrastructure. Focus on practical security analysis, threat assessment, and providing actionable insights for the telecom industry.
